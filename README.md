## 1. Project Overview
Kozy Brand was founded by two young creatives with a shared vision: to express freedom and promote positive living through fashion. They believed that living “Kozy and free” meant elevating your lifestyle while staying true to comfort — and that’s where the Kozy story began.


## 2. Website Goals and Objectives
1.  **Brand Awareness**: Introduce visitors to the KOZY brand, mission, and story.
2.  **Product Showcase**: Display the current product range with images and descriptions.
3.  **Lead Generation**: Capture business enquiries for wholesale, collaborations, and custom orders through the Enquiry form.
4.  **Customer Support**: Provide store locations, contact details, and a contact form for customer queries.

## 3. Target Audience
The target audience is South African youth aged 16-30 who are interested in streetwear, comfort fashion, and local brands. Secondary audience includes retail stores looking for wholesale opportunities.
