About KOZY

Our History

Kozy Brand was founded by two young creatives with a shared vision: to express freedom and promote positive living through fashion. They believed that living “Kozy and free” meant elevating your lifestyle while staying true to comfort and that's where the Kozy story began. From the start, we knew the look and feel of our clothing would define our voice. At Kozy, it's not just about how you look it's about how you feel. We create athleisure that blends comfort, style, and individuality for people who are always on the go but never lose sight of their well-being. In a world that rarely slows down, we're here to remind you to keep it Kozy, to feel good, live well, and express yourself authentically.

Mission

Our mission is to create a brand that blends stylish, cozy aesthetics with sustainable practices — offering more than just clothing, but an experience filled with warmth, comfort, and positive energy.

Vision

At Kozy, we strive to empower individuals to express themselves freely, embrace their unique journey, and feel good in what they wear — inside and out. Every piece we create is designed to support a lifestyle rooted in authenticity, mindfulness, and ease.

Our Values

  • Local Pride - Designed and inspired by Polokwane street culture
  • Affordable Quality - Premium without the R1000+ price tag
